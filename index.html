How to play

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on touch. Your spells fire on their own.

Runes: enemies drop glowing words. Walk over them to collect.

Forge: after each wave, pick any two words. They become a brand-new spell. Storm + Glass is not Storm + Bee. Experiment.

Wild runes ✦ let you type any word.

Level up: kills give XP. Each level, pick one upgrade: health, damage, more projectiles, cast speed and more.

Bosses arrive on waves 6, 11 and 16. On wave 20 the Unwriter wakes. Watch for red markers on the ground.

Every spell you discover is kept forever in your grimoire, and you can start runs with it. A new curse twists the rules every day.
